Gather Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Gather is a household meal planning app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is used, and how to contact us.

Data We Collect

  • Account and sign-in data, such as an anonymous or authenticated Firebase user identifier.
  • Household data, such as household name, household members, meal preferences, allergies, dislikes, and portion settings.
  • Meal planning data, such as recipes, recipe ingredients, planner entries, inventory items, grocery lists, notes, and shopping preferences.
  • App diagnostics and usage events, such as screen views, crashes, and performance signals from Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Device and app technical data, such as app version, device model, operating system version, and crash logs.

How We Use Data

  • To provide meal planning, household inventory, grocery list, and recipe features.
  • To personalize recommendations to household preferences and dietary constraints.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.
  • To support testing, customer support, and release quality checks.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase services for authentication, analytics, diagnostics, and crash reporting. If the app connects to a grocery provider or checkout flow, relevant grocery list or shopping data may be sent only as needed to provide that feature.

Retention

We keep app data for as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Test data may be removed after internal or closed testing.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can request deletion of your Gather app data by following the instructions on the Data Deletion page.
