Gather Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Gather Meal Planner (Gather) is a household meal planning app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is used, and how to contact us.

Developer contact: Gentle Signals Studio, gentlesignals.studio@gmail.com.

Data We Collect

  • Account and sign-in data, including your Firebase user identifier, email address and display name. Google sign-in does not give Gather your Google password. Invited email accounts authenticate with a password through Firebase Authentication; the Gather backend does not store that password.
  • Household data, such as household name, household members, meal preferences, allergies, dislikes, and portion settings.
  • Meal planning data, such as recipes, recipe ingredients, planner entries, inventory items, grocery lists, notes, and shopping preferences.
  • App diagnostics and usage events, such as screen views, crashes, and performance signals from Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Device and app technical data, such as app version, device model, operating system version, app-instance and advertising identifiers, and crash logs. Firebase Analytics derives approximate location from masked IP addresses; Gather does not request precise device location.
  • Text resulting from optional inventory dictation. Speech recognition is performed by the device's speech service, which may process audio under its own terms. Gather does not store raw audio.

How We Use Data

  • To provide meal planning, household inventory, grocery list, and recipe features.
  • To personalize recommendations to household preferences and dietary constraints.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.
  • To support testing, customer support, and release quality checks.

Health and Nutrition

Gather may help plan meals around dietary preferences, allergies, and nutrition-related choices. Gather is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase services for authentication, analytics, diagnostics, and crash reporting. If the app connects to a grocery provider or checkout flow, relevant grocery list or shopping data may be sent only as needed to provide that feature.

Meal planning and other assisted features send the information needed for the task, such as household member names and identifiers, meal preferences, dietary constraints, ingredient lists and recipe candidates, to OpenAI for processing. Your private household information is available to members of your household as needed for shared planning. Retailer passwords, cookies and login sessions remain on your device and are not sent to Gather's planning service.

Retention

We keep app data for as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Test data may be removed after internal or closed testing.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can request deletion of your Gather account and associated data by following the instructions on the Account and Data Deletion page. The same link is available in the app under Profile, Account and family invitations, Privacy and support. You do not need to sign in to make a request.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: gentlesignals.studio@gmail.com
