Gather Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 28, 2026

You can request deletion of your Gather Meal Planner account and its associated data by emailing gentlesignals.studio@gmail.com. You do not need to install the app or sign in. This is a support request, handled by Gentle Signals Studio, rather than an immediate automated deletion.

Developer contact: Gentle Signals Studio, gentlesignals.studio@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • The email address or Google account used for testing, if any.
  • Your household name or tester identifier, if known.
  • A short note saying that you want your Gather account and associated data deleted, or identifying particular data you want removed.

Please send the request from the email used to sign in, when possible, so we can verify ownership. Never send your password. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us for help verifying the account.

What We Delete

For account deletion requests, we delete your Gather account, its Firebase sign-in identity for Gather, and associated personal data, including member profiles, dietary preferences, inventory, personal recipes, planner, grocery list and related app records. We remove your household memberships and revoke Gather access. Shared records needed by other household members may remain with your personal information removed. Deleting Gather does not delete your Google account or an account you hold with a grocery retailer. We confirm completion by email.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups, or debugging records that cannot reasonably identify you. Backup copies expire according to normal backup retention schedules.
