Gather Data Deletion

Last updated: June 18, 2026

You can request deletion of your Gather app data by emailing gentlesignals.studio@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • The email address or Google account used for testing, if any.
  • Your household name or tester identifier, if known.
  • A short note saying that you want your Gather data deleted.

What We Delete

We will delete or anonymize app data associated with your account where reasonably identifiable, including household, inventory, recipe, planner, grocery list, and related app records.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups, or debugging records that cannot reasonably identify you. Backup copies expire according to normal backup retention schedules.
